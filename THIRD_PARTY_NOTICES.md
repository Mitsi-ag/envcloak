## EFF Large Wordlist for Passphrases

- Used in: `crates/envcloak-core/src/wordlists/eff_large.rs`, the words of a suggested passphrase.
- Source: the Electronic Frontier Foundation, <https://www.eff.org/dice>; file <https://www.eff.org/files/2016/07/18/eff_large_wordlist.txt>, SHA-256 `addd35536511597a02fa0a9ff1e5284677b8883b83e986e43f15a3db996b903e`.
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 United States (CC BY 3.0 US), <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/>.
- Changes: the dice numbers are dropped; the 7,776 words are kept in order.

## Pwdb-Public common passwords

- Used in: `crates/envcloak-core/src/wordlists/common_passwords.rs`, passwords a new passphrase may not be.
- Source: the Pwdb-Public top 1,000,000 passwords, <https://github.com/ignis-sec/Pwdb-Public>, as redistributed in SecLists, <https://github.com/danielmiessler/SecLists/blob/12274c98fdebe98c7a7284914436a472ed469aed/Passwords/Common-Credentials/Pwdb_top-1000000.txt>, SHA-256 `e9a88f67aafe65496682dc374559ee714e978bee50314767494c3e37a18c9fc8`.
- Changes: only entries of 12 or more printable ASCII characters are kept, lowercased, without duplicates, sorted.
